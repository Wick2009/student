### Featured Project — UESL Accessibility Initiative

One of the projects I'm most proud of is my work on the website for **UESL (Universal Esports League)**, a nonprofit dedicated to running an esports league for people with disabilities. Our team took an existing website and rebuilt it to be genuinely inclusive — not just technically compliant, but actually usable for everyone.

My main contributions:

**AI Chatbot**
I built and integrated a chatbot directly into the site so visitors can quickly get answers without having to navigate complex menus or walls of text. For users with cognitive or physical disabilities, reducing friction like this makes a real difference — instead of hunting through pages, they can just ask.

**IDD-Specific Accessibility Features**
I added a set of accessibility features specifically designed for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) — a group that's often overlooked in standard WCAG compliance work. This included simplified language toggles, clearer visual hierarchy, and interaction patterns tuned for users who may struggle with dense or ambiguous interfaces.

This project brought together everything I care about: coding, AI, and making technology work for people who are typically left behind by it.
